I am a postdoctoral researcher @ [ANSES](https://www.anses.fr/en) (French Agency for Food, Environmental and Occupational Health & Safety) and [INRAE](https://maiage.inrae.fr/), working on the [SafeFood4ClimDiet project](https://sitesv2.anses.fr/en/minisite/safefood4climdiet/safe-food-4-clim-diet). I currently work on *zero-shot named entity recognition in french/english for specialized domain*, the goal of the project being to identify new food trends/pratices driven by climate change. 

Previously, I was a PhD student @  [LISN Lab (Université Paris Saclay, CNRS)](https://www.lisn.fr/), supervised by [Patrick Paroubek](https://perso.limsi.fr/pap/) and [Thierry Hamon](https://perso.limsi.fr/hamon/). I worked on 🏥✅*clinical trials automatic quality assessment*, 📑*structured information extraction*, 📃*few-shot learning*, 💭*reasoning*, 💊*biomedical domain adaptation* and *data selection*.
